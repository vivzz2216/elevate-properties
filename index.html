SITE ARCHAEOLOGY

We listen before we sketch. Every topography carries rock formations and light angles that dictate the building's geometry.

MONOLITHIC WEIGHT

Defying gravity requires monumental mass. Deep cantilevers and cast-in-place concrete spine walls anchor structures against coastal winds.

CHIAROSCURO

Light has no power without shadow. We sculpt rooms around dusk, where indirect cove illumination meets the fading horizon.

TIME AS MATERIAL

We reject transient finishes that need synthetic upkeep. Honed stone, weathered bronze, and charred cedar grow more beautiful over decades.

THE HORIZON HOUSE

Costa Brava, Spain

Perched cantilevered above the Mediterranean coastline, The Horizon House dissolves the distinction between indoor comfort and elemental nature. Cast-in-place board-formed concrete walls frame panoramic twilight views, while thermal double-glazed pivot glass walls open to an infinity lap pool.

Typology Private Coastal Estate
Internal Area 820 m² / 8,820 sq ft
Materials Board-formed Concrete, Roman Travertine, Low-Iron Glass
Completion 2025
